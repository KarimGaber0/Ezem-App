Your data in Ezem

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 23 October 2026

Ezem (اِعْزِمْ) is a task-planning and focus app developed and operated by Athr Apps (Karim Gaber). This policy explains how the Ezem app and this public website handle your information, including Google sign-in and optional cloud backups. Contact: ezem.app.support@gmail.com.

You can use Ezem's local features without a cloud account. Signing in and uploading a backup are separate actions. The current cloud version offers manual snapshots; live synchronization is not yet implemented.

1. Information stored on your device

Local app data can include your tasks and notes, folders, reminders, completion history, focus-session records, settings, and a local profile name and gender if you provide them. Optional prayer-time features can use a selected city or approximate coordinates, which are stored locally. Ezem does not include an advertising SDK, usage-analytics SDK, or crash-report upload in the current implementation.

Device operating systems, enabled device-backup services, files you export, and widgets have their own storage behavior. Exported backups and widgets can contain task content. Removing the app may remove your local data.

2. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose Continue with Google, Google authenticates you and returns account information to our authentication provider, Supabase. This can include your Google account identifier, email address and its verification status, and basic profile information such as your display name and profile image.

We use this information to authenticate you, associate your cloud backups with your account, secure access, and handle account support. Ezem does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or Google contacts. Your Google password is entered with Google, not Ezem.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. We do not share it with unrelated third parties for their independent use.

3. Optional cloud backups

When you select Back up now, Ezem uploads a snapshot to Supabase. A snapshot contains task and folder content, completion history, finished focus sessions, retained deletion markers, your local profile fields, and supported portable date/time preferences. Account ownership and snapshot identifiers and timestamps are stored with the snapshot.

Cloud snapshots exclude the running timer, in-progress focus sessions, authentication tokens, device-specific sync cursors, operating-system preferences, and prayer coordinates. Local JSON exports may include more device preferences than cloud snapshots. Downloading a snapshot does not automatically restore it: restoration uses the app's import flow.

4. Providers and disclosure

  • Google processes Google sign-in under its Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase provides authentication and database storage for cloud accounts and backups. Our current database region is Frankfurt, Germany. Operational services and subprocessors may process information in other countries. See Supabase's Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this informational website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies to this website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Support email: if you contact us, your email address and message are processed to respond to the request. Do not include your password or access tokens.

Service providers may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request/security logs, to operate and protect their services. Data may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address fraud or security incidents. Any disclosure of Google user data remains subject to Google's applicable user-data requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

The cloud backup service retains at most the latest three snapshots per account, each limited to 1,000,000 bytes. Older snapshots are removed from active app storage when replaced by newer uploads. You can delete all active cloud snapshots in Settings → Backup → Cloud backups. This does not delete your local tasks or authentication account.

Your cloud authentication account is retained until deleted. To request deletion of your cloud account and associated backups, email ezem.app.support@gmail.com with the subject “Ezem data deletion”, using the account email if possible. We will verify ownership before carrying out the request. You can also delete your cloud account directly in Settings → Backup → Cloud backups → Delete cloud account after signing in and confirming. See our dedicated account and data deletion page. Deleting the authentication account removes its associated app backup records.

Local data remains under your control on your device. Signing out does not erase it. You can manage local items, export your data, or remove app data using your device's controls. Revoking Ezem's access in your Google account connections stops that authorization but does not itself delete copies already stored in Ezem or Supabase. Provider security logs, recovery copies, and legally required records may be retained according to applicable provider policies and legal obligations.

6. Security

Cloud requests use HTTPS. Access to app backup records is restricted to the authenticated owner by server-side database policies. In the current app implementation, authentication sessions and temporary PKCE sign-in verifiers stay in memory and are excluded from app exports; restarting the app requires sign-in again. Cloud snapshots are not end-to-end encrypted, and no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stay in local mode, choose when to upload, download and delete cloud snapshots, revoke Google authorization, and request access, correction, or deletion by emailing the developer. Privacy rights depend on applicable law. We may request information needed to verify your identity and protect other users before responding.

Children and age-appropriate access

Ezem supports local use by children and older users. Before optional Google sign-in, a neutral age screen asks for an age group without preselecting an answer. For users aged 12 or younger, cloud sign-in and cloud backups are unavailable. Local tasks, profiles and optional prayer coordinates remain on the device. The age choice stays only in memory for the current app session and is not sent to the cloud or included in exports. Ezem has no ads, analytics, or crash-report uploads.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when data practices change and revise the date above. If we introduce a new use of Google user data, we will provide the required notice and obtain consent before using it for that purpose.

For privacy, support, or account-deletion requests, contact Karim Gaber at ezem.app.support@gmail.com.

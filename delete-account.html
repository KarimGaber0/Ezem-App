Delete your Ezem account and data

23 October 2026

Request deletion of your Ezem cloud account and associated backups here, even if the app is no longer installed. Developer: Athr Apps (Karim Gaber).

Request deletion from the web

Email us from the address used for Google sign-in, if possible. Use the subject “Ezem account deletion” and ask us to delete your account and associated data. Never send passwords, access codes, or the contents of your tasks.

Request account and data deletion

Email: e.karimgaber@gmail.com. We verify account ownership to protect your data, delete the account and backups, and confirm completion by email.

Delete directly in Ezem

  1. Open Settings → Backup → Cloud backups.
  2. Sign in with the Google account linked to Ezem.
  3. Choose “Delete cloud account”, read the confirmation, and confirm deletion.

What is deleted and what stays?

Deletion permanently removes your Ezem cloud authentication account, its identity profile, and associated cloud backups, including task content, history, finished focus sessions and profile fields contained in those snapshots. Your local tasks, profile, and exported files remain on your devices; use your device’s app-data controls if you want to erase local app data. Your Google account is not deleted.

You can also email the address above to request backup-only deletion; state clearly that you want to keep your account. To remove only backups in the app while keeping the account, choose “Delete cloud backups”. Providers may retain security logs and recovery copies according to their policies and legal obligations. We do not retain active cloud snapshots after account deletion. See the privacy policy for details.

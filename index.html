Your choice, your data

Local by default.
Cloud when you choose.

Your core task data stays on your device in local mode. You can export and restore your own JSON backups.

Cloud-enabled builds offer Google sign-in and manual snapshot backups. The current cloud version is being tested; automatic cross-device synchronization is not yet available.

Why Google sign-in?

Google identifies your account so Ezem can keep your cloud backups associated with you. Ezem requests basic identity, email, and profile information. It does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or contacts.

Cloud uploads happen when you choose Back up now. The service keeps up to three snapshots, with a 1 MB limit for each.
